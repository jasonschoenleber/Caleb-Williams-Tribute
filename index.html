    Kings of the comeback

    Six fourth-quarter comebacks in 2025 led the NFL and tied Peyton Manning for the most in a season by a player under 25. Seven counting the playoffs.

    Wild Card vs. Green Bay

    3.0%

    Chicago's lowest win probability. From late in the second quarter until 2:59 remained, it never climbed above 25%. Williams threw for 361 yards, a Bears playoff record, and the Bears won 31-27 after trailing 21-3 at half.

    Week 16 vs. Green Bay

    3%

    Down 16-6 with 5:03 left. He forced overtime and won it on a 46-yard TD to DJ Moore, later named Next Gen Stats Moment of the Year.

    Week 9 at Cincinnati

    +58.7

    His 58-yard TD to Colston Loveland with 17 seconds left moved win probability from 39.8% to 98.5%, the second-biggest single-play swing of the season.

        Honors and recognition

        What the league, the players and the community have said with awards.

        Madden NFL 27 cover

        The first player in Bears history on a standard Madden cover, entering 2026 rated 90 overall.

        NFL Top 100: No. 51

        Voted by fellow players in his debut on the list, the highest ranking ever for a Bears quarterback.

        NGS Moment of the Year

        The overtime walk-off to DJ Moore against Green Bay, honored at NFL Honors in February 2026.

        NFC Offensive Player of the Week

        Week 3, 2025 vs. Dallas: 298 yards, 4 TDs, no sacks, and a career-best 142.6 rating.

        NFLPA Community MVP

        Recognized for the Caleb Cares Foundation, which brings dozens of Chicago kids to every home game.

        NFC North champions

        11-6 in 2025, the division title and the Bears' first playoff win since the 2010 season.

        "He rises to the occasion time and time again."Ben Johnson, Bears head coach
        "The best throw until he makes the next best throw."Rome Odunze, on the 4th-and-8 Wild Card throw
        "This is absolutely one of the great throws in NFL history."Dan Orlovsky, ESPN
        "You won't outrun me during the game. So just run."Williams, to Luther Burden III before the 62.1-air-yard TD

        Room to grow

        His 58.1% completion rate in 2025 ranked near the bottom of the league. Part of that was on his receivers: Next Gen Stats had him losing more passing yards to drops than any other QB, enough to have made him Chicago's first 4,000-yard passer.
